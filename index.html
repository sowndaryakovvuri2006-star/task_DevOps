Radhakrishna

About Radhakrishna

Radhakrishna is a revered deity in Hinduism, symbolizing the divine love between Lord Krishna and Radha. Their story is an eternal symbol of devotion and spiritual union.

Significance

The divine couple Radha and Krishna are celebrated in many scriptures, art, music, and dance forms, representing the joy of divine love and devotion.
